Field Almanac · No. 13

Level 13 Prep

A ten-week CogAT practice route for seventh graders, with a parent-run timed mock every Saturday. The interface is built as a surveyor's almanac: a numbered route, ruled entries, figures set tabular, and gold used only as a stroke. Foundations are below; the working prototype follows.

Three strand colors, used only for their strand: flare for verbal, lagoon for quantitative, violet for nonverbal. Correct and incorrect are never red and green — correct fills with its strand color, incorrect gets a hollow ring and an underline. Nothing in the app is alarm-colored.
