Why we built it

Most families prepping for a gifted-program test end up with one of two things: an expensive tutor, or a stack of static worksheets that repeat the same questions every time. Neither tells you what to do today, and neither adjusts when your kid gets faster than the material.

How it actually works

Four things it does that a workbook can't

A real day-by-day plan
Ten weeks, seventy short lessons, mapped to when your district actually tests — not a random question generator.
Never the same set twice
Hundreds of questions per topic, drawn fresh every session, so your kid is reasoning through it, not remembering it from last week.
One login, every kid

Timed practice exams are parent-started, with a visible countdown — nobody sits a full mock by accident.
