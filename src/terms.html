We do our best to keep the material accurate and age-appropriate, but if you spot something wrong, please let us know.

3. Passes and payments

Two kinds of passes are offered (Stripe processes the payments; see the Privacy Policy §4):

  • Individual Pass — one-time. Unlocks one student's full 10-week program and mock tests for the current admissions season.
  • Family Pass — one-time or annual. Unlocks every student on the roster for the current season, or on an auto-renewing annual subscription that you can cancel any time from Parent Settings.

A "season" runs from the day of purchase through the end of the following school year's fall CogAT testing window (roughly October of the next calendar year). Prices are shown at checkout in USD.
