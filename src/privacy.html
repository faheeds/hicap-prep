Last updated: September 14, 2026.

This policy explains exactly what HiCap Prep stores about the parent who signs up and about the kids whose CogAT practice is tracked. It's written specifically for this app, not copied from a template — if something below no longer matches how the app behaves, that's a bug to file, not a feature we're hiding.

Short version. One parent account per family. The only thing we store about a kid is a first name, an emoji avatar, their grade level, and their quiz history on this app. Nothing about your kid ever leaves your family's private data unless you export it yourself. You can delete everything from Parent Settings at any time.

1. What we collect

1.1 About the parent (the account holder)

  • Email address (required to sign in).
  • A hashed parent PIN — the digits are never sent to us; only a one-way PBKDF2 hash is stored, and the check runs entirely on our server.
  • Timestamps for sign-up, last sign-in, and the parental-consent confirmation described in §5.

1.2 About each child on the roster

  • A first name (or nickname — whatever the parent types in).
  • An emoji avatar the parent picks from a fixed list.
  • A US grade level (1 or 3–11) and its matching CogAT level.
  • The child's practice history on this app: which sessions they completed, how many questions they got right, which questions they missed, their current streak, and any badges they've earned.

We do not ask for or store: the child's last name, date of birth, home address, phone number, school, photograph, voice, or any real-world identifier beyond the first name the parent chose.

2. What we do not collect

  • No advertising or third-party analytics trackers. There is no Google Analytics, Facebook pixel, ad-network SDK, or session replay of any kind on this app.
  • No location data.
  • No cookies used for tracking — the only browser storage in use is the Supabase Auth session token and (in local-only mode) a cached copy of the family's roster so the app still works without a network connection.
  • No device fingerprinting.

3. Who can see this data

  • The parent who owns the account, when signed in.
  • Our database at Supabase enforces this at the row level: every row (families, students, attempts, badges) is tagged with the family's owner, and the database refuses to return rows to any request that isn't from that owner's signed-in session.
  • The HiCap Prep operator (this app's maintainer) has technical access to the database for maintenance and support, and will only look at a specific family's data if you email us and ask for help with your account.

We do not sell or share the data with anyone else. There are no ad networks or "data partners" in our stack.

4. Third-party service providers

The app is built on top of these services. Each one processes only what it needs to do its job, and each one has been chosen because it has a clear commitment to not sell customer data:

  • Supabase — hosts the database and the sign-in system. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Stripe (when paid features ship) — processes payments for the seasonal Family Pass. Stripe receives the parent's email and payment details; it never receives any information about the kids or their quiz history.

5. Parental consent (COPPA)

HiCap Prep is designed for use by kids under 13 with a parent involved. Because of that, we require the parent to explicitly consent to their child's data being collected as described here, before any child data is entered. The consent step is a checkbox shown after sign-up and its timestamp is stored on the family's account row.

The parent can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the account (see §7), which erases every row belonging to that family from our systems.

6. Data retention

Family data stays on our systems as long as the family is actively using the app. If a family's account has been inactive (no sign-in and no practice sessions) for 18 months, we mark the account for deletion and — once we've integrated email notifications — will send a warning to the parent's email at least 60 days before the deletion runs. Deletion is a hard delete: every row belonging to the family, including the parent's auth account, is removed.

7. Deleting your family's data

Sign in as the parent → Parent area → Settings → "Delete my family's account". Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no soft delete, no 30-day grace period, and no way for us to bring it back. That's deliberate; when you ask for the data to be gone, it's gone.

8. Exporting your family's data

Sign in as the parent → Parent area → Settings → "Download JSON" or "Download CSV". The JSON export includes every field we have on your family (except password hashes and auth tokens). The CSV export is a spreadsheet-friendly row per practice session.

9. Changes to this policy

If we materially change what data is collected or who can see it, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and surface a notice inside the app the next time you sign in. Cosmetic edits (fixing a typo, reformatting) may happen silently.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your family's data: email the address listed on the app's landing page, or open an issue on the app's public repository if one is linked from that page.
